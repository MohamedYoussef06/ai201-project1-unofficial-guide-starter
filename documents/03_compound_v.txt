SOURCE: Forum explainer thread — "What is Compound V? Everything we know"
TYPE: Lore / worldbuilding explainer

Compound V is the fictional chemical at the heart of The Boys. It is the substance that creates
supes. The major lore reveal early in the series is that NO superhero in this world is naturally born
with powers — Vought International secretly injects babies with Compound V, and the "miracle birth"
origin stories the public believes are corporate marketing lies.

Key facts fans cite about Compound V:
- It was developed by Vought, with roots tracing back to Nazi scientist Frederick Vought during WWII.
- Injected into infants, it produces unpredictable results: some children get powers, some are
  disfigured or die. Survivors become marketable supes.
- Vought hides the existence of Compound V from the public to maintain the myth that supes are
  naturally gifted, which is more profitable and easier to control.

Temp V (Temporary Compound V) is a later variant that gives a normal adult superpowers for roughly
24 hours. It is used by members of the Boys, notably Butcher and Kimiko-adjacent storylines, and is
eventually revealed to cause fatal brain lesions with repeated use.

The exposure of Compound V to the public is a turning point: it shatters the illusion that supes are
gods and reframes them as manufactured products of a pharmaceutical-entertainment conglomerate.
