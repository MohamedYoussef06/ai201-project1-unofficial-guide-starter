SOURCE: Fan analysis — "Soldier Boy and Stormfront: the show's political villains"
TYPE: Character / theme analysis

Stormfront (introduced in Season 2) is a social-media-savvy supe who is eventually revealed to be a
literal Nazi — originally named Klara Risinger, she was one of the first Compound V test subjects under
Frederick Vought in WWII Germany. She survived for decades and reinvented herself as a hip, meme-driven
hero. Fans highlight her as the show's sharpest satire of how white-supremacist ideology launders
itself through internet culture and youth-friendly branding. She seduces Homelander into her racist
ideology of building a "master race" of supes before being burned and dismembered by Maeve, Starlight,
and Kimiko.

Soldier Boy (introduced in Season 3) is a parody of Captain America. Marketed as America's first
superhero and a WWII icon, he is in reality a cruel, cowardly bully who got his own team killed and was
secretly captured by Russians, who used him as a test subject. He emits a powerful radioactive blast
from his chest that can strip other supes of their powers — making him one of the few weapons capable
of hurting Homelander. He is also revealed to be Homelander's biological father, deepening the show's
theme of inherited abuse and toxic masculinity passed down through generations.

Both characters exist to draw a line from old American mythmaking to present-day extremism.
