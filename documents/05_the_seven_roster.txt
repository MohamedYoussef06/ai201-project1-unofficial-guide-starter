SOURCE: Fan-maintained roster guide — "The Seven, member by member"
TYPE: Reference / roster

The Seven is Vought's flagship superhero team, an in-universe parody of the Justice League. Membership
changes across seasons as members die, defect, or are fired for PR reasons.

Core/long-running members fans reference:
- Homelander: leader, Superman parody, flight + heat vision + super strength. The most powerful and
  most dangerous member.
- Queen Maeve: Wonder Woman parody, super strength and durability. One of the few members with a
  conscience; her arc is about disillusionment and eventually trying to take Homelander down.
- A-Train: the fastest man alive, a Flash parody. Introduced as the villain who kills Hughie's
  girlfriend Robin by running through her at super speed. His Compound V abuse damages his heart.
- The Deep: aquatic hero (Aquaman parody) who can talk to sea creatures. Used heavily for comic relief
  and as a satire of disgraced celebrities seeking redemption; involved in the show's #MeToo themes.
- Black Noir: silent, masked martial-arts expert. A mystery-box character whose backstory becomes a
  major reveal.
- Starlight (Annie January): the audience's entry point into the Seven, a young idealistic hero whose
  faith in the institution is destroyed almost immediately.

Later additions fans discuss include Stormfront and Soldier Boy, both of whom carry heavy thematic
weight (see separate notes). The Seven functions less as a team and more as a managed celebrity brand.
