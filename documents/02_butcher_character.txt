SOURCE: Fan wiki character profile — Billy Butcher
TYPE: Character analysis

Billy Butcher is the leader of "the Boys," the vigilante crew that hunts corrupt supes. He is a
former British special-forces operative, foul-mouthed, ruthless, and driven almost entirely by hatred
of Homelander and of Vought. His catchphrase energy is summed up by lines like "diabolical" and his
habit of calling people "c**t" affectionately.

Butcher's core motivation is the disappearance of his wife, Becca, whom he believes Homelander raped.
This personal vendetta makes him willing to cross almost any moral line: he lies to his own team,
manipulates Hughie, and is willing to sacrifice innocent people to get at supes. Fans frequently
debate whether Butcher is an antihero or simply a different flavor of monster than the supes he hunts.

A major arc fans discuss is Butcher taking Temp V — a temporary version of Compound V that grants
powers for about 24 hours but is later revealed to be slowly killing its users. This puts Butcher in
the position of becoming the very thing he hates. His internal conflict between protecting Becca's son
Ryan and using him as a weapon against Homelander is one of the show's central tensions.

Butcher's relationship with Hughie is often described as a toxic father-son or mentor dynamic: Butcher
weaponizes Hughie's grief and pulls him deeper into violence, but also occasionally shows real care.
