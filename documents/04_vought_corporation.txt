SOURCE: Wiki overview — Vought International
TYPE: Worldbuilding / corporate satire

Vought International is the megacorporation that manufactures, manages, and monetizes superheroes in
The Boys. It is the show's central vehicle for satire: superheroes are treated as intellectual
property, brand assets, and marketing tools rather than as genuine protectors.

Vought controls the Seven, runs blockbuster superhero movies (the "Vought Cinematic Universe," a
parody of the MCU), sells merchandise, and lobbies the U.S. government to get supes into the military.
The company's real product is image management: they spin every supe disaster, cover up collateral
deaths, and script "heroic" moments for the cameras.

Fans highlight Vought as the true villain of the series — more than any individual supe. The company
created Compound V, hid it, and built an entire economy on lies. Stan Edgar, Vought's CEO for much of
the series, is frequently praised by fans as the most genuinely intimidating character precisely
because he has no powers; he controls Homelander through pure corporate leverage and contempt.

Recurring satirical targets via Vought include: corporate diversity-washing (the "Brave Maeve" and
later pride-month branding), the entertainment-industrial complex, and the way real harm gets buried
under PR. Vought Tower is the corporate headquarters and a frequent setting.
