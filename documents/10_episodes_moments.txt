TYPE: Episode/plot reference

A digest of pivotal plot moments fans most often reference when discussing The Boys:

- Pilot ("The Name of the Game"): A-Train kills Robin, recruiting Hughie into the story; the Deep
  assaults Starlight on her first day in the Seven. The episode establishes that heroes are monsters.
- The "Translucent" arc: the Boys capture the invisible supe Translucent, whose skin is bulletproof
  carbon; they ultimately kill him by detonating a bomb inside him, marking Hughie's first kill.
- Flight 37: Homelander and Maeve fail to save a hijacked passenger plane, then Homelander chooses to
  let everyone die and spins it into pro-Vought propaganda. A defining "Homelander is the villain"
  moment.
- The boat/whale scene: the Boys drive a speedboat through the Deep's whale "Lucy," a darkly comic and
  infamous set piece.
- Herogasm: a notorious supe orgy event that becomes the title and setting of a chaotic, ultra-violent
  Season 3 episode, including the Soldier Boy power-draining blast.
- The Stormfront fight: Maeve, Starlight, and Kimiko beat Stormfront down, after which Homelander's
  son Ryan accidentally kills her with his own laser eyes.
- Season finales repeatedly center on whether anyone can actually stop Homelander, who grows more
  openly authoritarian and less interested in hiding his contempt for humans as the show progresses.

These moments are the most common reference points in fan discussion and "best episode" debates.
